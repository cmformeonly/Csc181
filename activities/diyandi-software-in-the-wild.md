## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
Locals and visitors who walk between Diyandi Festival events, especially the parades.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
They walk a lot in the crowds so it's easy for them to lose something like a phone, bag, wallet, and other valuable items.

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
They want to enjoy the events and if they lose something, they want to find out if someone turned it in without spending the whole day searching.

---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
Based on my observation during the festival, there were no lost and found desk or volunteer during the events. Someone who finds an item might not know where to take it and the owner has no place to ask for lost items.

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
A simple mobile-friendly Diyandi Lost and Found website.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
Anyone who finds a lost item can post and share a short description. People who lost something can check the website if someone found their valuable. If they lost their phone, they can ask a friend to check for them. The site would not give away all the details of the item. Users can send a request to claim the item and if the description matches, it's up to the finder to meet them.

---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. Post a found item with a short description.
2. Search found items by type like bag, phone, wallet, etc. Users can send a request to claim the item.

---

## 6. Important qualities

Identify **two qualities** that would make your proposed system useful. You may consider whether it should be easy to use, fast, reliable, safe, private, accessible, multilingual, low-data, clear, or available during high demand.

### Quality 1: Low data use

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Not everyone has unlimited data, and signal is often weak during the festival so the website stays small and should still load on a weak signal.

### Quality 2: Easy to use

**Why does this matter to users?**  
Some people are not used to mobile phones, so it needs few buttons and big text in English, Tagalog, or Cebuano.

---

## 7. How to tell whether the solution helped

How could you determine whether your proposed solution actually helped users?

Compare the number of items posted to the number of claims by the end of the festival. Ask users who have claimed lost items if the digital tool made their lives easier compared to helplessly searching the whole day finding lost items.
